# ZAVILO AI 🤖

> Your notification-aware AI assistant that replies like YOU.

Zavilo is not just another chatbot. It's a licensed Android system that reads your notifications and understands your context.

### ⚡ What it does
- Listens to incoming notifications (WhatsApp, Instagram, etc)
- Understands context and intent
- Learns how YOU talk
- Auto-generates replies in your style

### 🔐 Licensed System
Zavilo runs on a device-lock system.
One license = One device.
Managed by `LicenseManager.kt`

> `ANDROID_ID` based activation
